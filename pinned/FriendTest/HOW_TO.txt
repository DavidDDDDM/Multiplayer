Multiplayer test for Dawson (about 5 minutes of your time, then a few minutes of waiting)

ONE-TIME SETUP (Steam Workshop, RimWorld closed):
 1. UNSUBSCRIBE the official "Multiplayer" mod if you have it (it clashes with this one).
 2. SUBSCRIBE: Harmony, Prepatcher, and "Multiplayer (Dawson pinned)" (this one).
 3. Make sure RimWorld is fully updated (same version as Dawson) and Steam is running.

THE TEST:
 4. Open this folder (Steam > Workshop > this item, or
    ...\steamapps\workshop\content\294100\<this item's number>\FriendTest\).
 5. Double-click run_replay.bat (if it shows as run_replay.txt, rename it to .bat first).
 6. RimWorld opens with a separate test profile, runs by itself, and closes. Don't click in it.
 7. A folder opens with files named "friend" and "friend2". Send both to Dawson. Done.

Your normal saves and mod list are not changed (the test uses %USERPROFILE%\rw-mp-test).
